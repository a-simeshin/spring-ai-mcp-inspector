src/App.tsx + src/lib/hooks/useIsCompactLayout.ts: compact
(<1024px, Tailwind `lg`) layout so the bottom History/Server
Notifications pane never overlays tab content. Upstream renders a
`flex h-screen` row: the config sidebar keeps its draggable inline
width at every viewport and the History pane keeps a fixed inline
height (useDraggablePane(300)), so at e.g. 780x437 only ~137px of
tab content remained visible under the pane and the Tools tab's
"List Tools" button sat geometrically under the pane header:
elementFromPoint at its centre returned the History/Clear div and
real clicks never reached the button. Local fix: a new
useIsCompactLayout hook (matchMedia "(max-width: 1023.98px)",
change-listener kept in sync) mirrors the CSS breakpoint for React;
the root container becomes `flex-col lg:flex-row` with a
`min-h-screen` guarantee (so Vite's `:root` background-color #242424
never shows under a short compact page); below `lg` the sidebar
drops its inline width (full-width block with a definite h-[50vh]
height, so the Sidebar's internal body column scrolls instead of the
whole block including its header) and does not mount the
desktop-only sidebar drag handle, so a compact pointer press on the
right edge cannot enter the drag path or mutate the hidden sidebar
width state; the History pane drops its fixed height and drag handle
and becomes an in-flow section with a definite h-[40vh] height (a
max-height-only wrapper would resolve the inner h-full chain to auto
and leave the History and Server Notifications columns without an
independently scrollable height); the root container's fixed
viewport height (h-screen) is desktop-only too, since 50vh + 40vh
caps would otherwise consume the whole compact viewport and squeeze
the tab column to zero height; the content column gains
min-w-0/min-h-0. Desktop (>=1024px) geometry: sidebar width drag,
resizable fixed-height pane, is unchanged. The CSS lg: classes alone
cannot remove the desktop-only inline styles, which is why the hook
is required. Covered by the @Nested ResponsiveHistoryLayout Selenide
group in spring-ai-mcp-inspector-demo-webmvc InspectorUiIT
(elementFromPoint at the List Tools centre at 780x437 with both drag
handles absent; the History and Server Notifications columns scroll
independently under real overflow; the tab panel and the History
pane have disjoint boxes and the document has no horizontal overflow
(scrollWidth <= document.documentElement.clientWidth) at 768x800 and
1023x768; drag handles present at 1024px; 375px regression of patch
8 with all 10 enabled tabs clickable) and by the useIsCompactLayout
jest unit tests.

src/lib/types/historyEntry.ts + src/lib/persistentHistory.ts +
 src/lib/hooks/useConnection.ts + src/App.tsx - persist inspector
 request/response history in localStorage under a single schema-versioned
 key `mcp-inspector.history.v1`, bucketed by connection id. History
 entries (JSON-RPC requests, responses, errors, and timestamps) survive
 page reload. Each connection bucket caps at 100 entries; the global
 store caps at 500 KB, with FIFO eviction of the oldest entries when
 limits are exceeded. The Clear button in the History panel clears both
 in-memory and persistent storage for the current connection. Entries
 for different saved connections are stored in separate buckets and do
 not mix. Unsaved sessions use the bucket key "ephemeral". Upstream had
 no persistent history at all: every page reload lost all recorded
 requests and responses. Covered by
 `src/lib/__tests__/persistentHistory.test.ts` (15 unit tests:
 serialization, eviction, per-connection isolation, global limit).

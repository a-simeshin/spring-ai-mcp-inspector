src/components/ResourcesTab.tsx + src/components/ListPane.tsx: the
Resources tab content grid (resources list, resource templates list,
preview pane) stacks into a single full-width column below the `md`
(768px) breakpoint instead of rendering three side-by-side columns at
every viewport. Upstream hard-codes `grid-cols-3`, so at a 375px phone
each pane gets about 110px: card titles truncate to "Resource T...",
the preview helper text breaks mid-word ("vie"/"w"), the List
Resources / List Templates labels touch the card borders and the
panes' inner scrollbars stick out of the cards. Local fix, companion
of the compact-layout, mobile-layout-grid and
history-notifications-mobile-stack patches (issue #58/#60 mobile
contract): the grid becomes `grid-cols-1 md:grid-cols-3` and gains a
`resources-content-grid` testid for Selenide geometry assertions;
`min-w-0` on the shared ListPane root and on the preview pane lets
each child shrink inside its grid cell so the inner scroll areas
never overflow the card borders; desktop (>=768px) three-column
geometry is unchanged. Covered by the Selenide InspectorUiIT updates
delivered with this change set: the new MobileViewport375x812
resourcesPanesStack_375x812 test (single column, pairwise disjoint
panes, no horizontal document overflow, buttons inside the viewport)
and the md 767x800/768x800 grid flip test, plus the jest pin
ResourcesTab "Responsive layout" (grid-cols-1 + md:grid-cols-3 +
resources-content-grid testid; min-w-0 on the pane roots asserted for
every tools-list-pane instance and for the preview pane, which gains a
resources-preview-pane testid as its jest anchor).

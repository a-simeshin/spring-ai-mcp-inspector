src/components/ToolsTab.tsx + src/components/ListPane.tsx + src/App.tsx
(+ src/components/__tests__/ToolsTab.test.tsx): responsive Tools
list/detail grid and stable testid anchors for the 375px mobile-layout
fix. Upstream hard-codes `grid grid-cols-2 gap-4` for the Tools
list/detail split, so at a 375px viewport the two columns overlap and
the tool list is unusable. Local fix: the grid becomes
`grid grid-cols-1 sm:grid-cols-2 gap-4` (one column below the `sm`
640px breakpoint, two columns at and above it) and the
geometry-critical nodes gain stable anchors: `tools-list-detail-grid`
and `tools-detail-pane` and `run-tool-button` (ToolsTab.tsx),
`tools-list-pane`, `search-button`, `search-input` (existing
`name="search"` kept) and `tool-row-${index}` (ListPane.tsx), and
`config-pane` on the App.tsx sidebar wrapper (testid only, no layout
change). Covered by the ToolsTab.test.tsx unit suite (grid classes +
testids) delivered with this change set and by the Selenide
ResponsiveLayout / ResponsiveToolsCriticalPath groups in InspectorUiIT
(geometry at 375x667, 639/640 grid flip, clickability of the six
canonical controls).

Viewport contract part (same patch): the accepted mobile contract
(issue #58, plan v10) requires the config/list/detail pane rectangles
to fit the 375x667 viewport at scrollY=0 after listing tools and
selecting a row. Below `sm` the sidebar cap drops from 50vh to 30vh
(App.tsx, `h-[30vh] sm:h-[50vh] lg:h-auto`) and the two Tools panes are
height-bounded with internal scrolling (`max-h-[24vh]` list,
`max-h-[17vh]` detail, both `sm:max-h-none sm:overflow-y-visible`) so
the stacked panes fit the first viewport instead of growing past the
fold; at and above `sm` the upstream natural heights are untouched.

src/components/Sidebar.tsx: show a toast notification when a connection
error is set, and dismiss it when the error clears (on retry or successful
connect). The toast uses the existing @radix-ui/react-toast system with
destructive variant, ensuring the error is immediately visible without
scrolling, complementing the inline role=alert in the sidebar. Also moved
the inline alert from the bottom of the sidebar config pane to the top,
right after the Transport Type selector, so both the toast and the inline
alert are visible at the top of the viewport on any screen size. Covered
by src/components/__tests__/Sidebar.connectFailureAlert.test.tsx.

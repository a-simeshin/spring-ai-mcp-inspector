Adds keep-alive observability to the Connection pane (#235, t_3925eae7).

The Sidebar shows "Last keep-alive: Xs ago" with a live ticking counter
(updated every second) when keep-alive pings have been observed. When no
pings have been seen since connect, a neutral "no keep-alive observed"
message is shown instead.

When the keep-alive stream goes stale (no pings for longer than 2x the
estimated interval, or for 90s when not a single ping has arrived since
connect), a warning banner appears explaining that the connection is likely
dead, probably due to an idle timeout on a load balancer or proxy (ALB,
nginx, typically 60s). The banner is rendered in both the observed and the
never-pinged branches, so a silent drop cannot hide behind the neutral
message, and it disappears automatically when pings resume.

App.tsx subscribes to the useKeepAlive hook and passes the state down to
Sidebar as props (keepAliveLastPingAt, keepAliveIsStale, keepAliveObserved).

Covered by Sidebar.keepAlive.test.tsx (9 tests: no keep-alive state,
ticking counter, stale banner shown/hidden (including the never-pinged
90s fallback branch), banner dismissed on resume).

src/App.tsx#readResource — fix stale right-pane after switching
between resources and templates, and fix Refresh / re-selecting a
row never re-reading the resource: the pane froze at the first-read
snapshot until a page reload. Upstream early-returns on cache hit
without syncing the singleton `resourceContent` state that
ResourcesTab renders, and never sends resources/read again. Local
fix: on cache hit, sync `resourceContent` from the cache and keep
going: resources/read is sent every time (stale-while-revalidate),
the response refreshes the pane and the cache, and cached error
payloads are retried the same way. Covered by
App.resourceRefresh.test.tsx and the demo E2E resource refresh IT.

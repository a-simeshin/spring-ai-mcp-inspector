src/App.tsx: mobile pre-connect sidebar height (issue
#230 remainder). Upstream (and the fork before this patch) kept the
vh caps (below-`sm` 30vh, `sm`..`lg` 50vh) on the config-pane
wrapper at all times; on a 375x812 phone the sidebar's inner scroll
area showed ~113px of a ~500px column, so the URL input (top ~193)
and the Connect button (top ~481) sat inside the inner scroll out of
view at scrollY=0 and a real click at Connect's centre hit the
floating theme bar (Connect stayed reachable only by scrolling the
sidebar's inner area). On an 812x375 landscape phone the 50vh cap
squeezed the pane to ~187px and put Connect back inside an inner
scroll. Local fix:
the wrapper's height classes become conditional on a live client
(mcpClient): pre-connect BOTH cap classes are dropped (no vh cap
below lg), so the sidebar renders at its natural height
and URL/Connect sit in normal flow (at 375x812 the whole pre-connect
block, header + form + footer, fits the viewport); once connected the
cap returns (`h-[30vh] sm:h-[50vh]`) so the accepted mobile contract
of issue #58 / plan v10 (config/list/detail pane rects inside the
first 667px viewport at scrollY=0 after listing tools) keeps holding
unchanged. Desktop (>=lg) geometry is untouched: the cap classes are
compact-only and `lg:h-auto` wins there as before. Covered by the
@Nested MobileViewport375x812 Selenide group in
spring-ai-mcp-inspector-demo-webmvc InspectorUiIT (real device
emulation 375x812 DPR 3 via CDP: pre-connect URL input and Connect
button fully inside the viewport at scrollY=0 with nothing overlaying
their centres, real click connects; landscape 812x375 device
emulation: no clipped inner scroll area on the wrapper, URL input
visible at scrollY=0, Connect reachable by document scroll alone;
post-connect the 30vh cap state is re-probed: panes still stack, no
horizontal overflow).

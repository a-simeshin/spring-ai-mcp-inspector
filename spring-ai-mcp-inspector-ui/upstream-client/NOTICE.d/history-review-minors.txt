src/App.tsx + src/lib/persistentHistory.ts (+ regression tests in
src/__tests__/App.transportScoping.test.tsx and
src/lib/__tests__/persistentHistory.test.ts): owner review minors on
PR #218 (2026-11-13). Three fixes in the history bucket keying:

- App.tsx historyKeyForConnection: a selected saved entry adopts the
  pending history bucket only while the form still points at its
  endpoint (the same transport-scoped comparison the disconnect-time
  carry-over uses). The association previously survived a reload, so
  typing a different URL after selecting A still wrote into A's
  bucket; a mismatched form now falls back to the transport-scoped
  ad-hoc key.
- App.tsx handleDeleteConnection: deleting the selected connection
  also releases the live session's frozen bucket key. Previously the
  frozen key kept receiving writes after clearHistory and recreated
  an orphan bucket for the deleted id; the session now keeps writing
  into the form-derived bucket.
- persistentHistory.ts migrateHistory: merged buckets are sorted by
  timestamp ascending (canonical appendHistory order) before the FIFO
  trim. A Save over an existing connection migrates a source bucket
  whose entries are older than the destination's newest; plain
  concatenation interleaved the two ascending sequences and the panel
  showed entries out of order.

Regression tests cover all three scenarios and are RED without the
fixes.
